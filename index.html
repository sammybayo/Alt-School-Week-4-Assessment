Biography

I'm Samuel, a mechanical engineer from Nigeria. I graduated with a First Class degree in Mechanical Engineering from Redeemer's University in Ede, and I'm a Graduate Member of the Nigerian Society of Engineers.

My hands-on experience comes from two SIWES placements in Lagos. At CIG Motors, I got close to real automotive work. At Greenview Development Nigeria Limited, I saw how a port terminal actually runs day to day. Those placements showed me how much engineering depends on good systems, good people, and good tools working together.

My interests cut across automotive, oil and gas, and technology, and I'm especially curious about the places where these fields overlap.

Why I Joined ALT School

My degree gave me a strong engineering foundation. What I want now is the technical and digital skill set that modern engineering teams expect, and a learning environment that pushes me to build things rather than just read about them.

  • To pair my mechanical engineering training with software and digital skills.
  • To learn by building real projects and getting feedback along the way.
  • To join a community of ambitious people who take their growth seriously.
  • To prepare for internships and early-career roles in a competitive market.
